Port Meridian — Open World Crime. An original top-down 2D open-world crime game built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty: a five-zone city (Downtown, Old Harbor, Neon Row, Sunset Beach, North Hills) to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a 0–5 star wanted level with police chases, roadblocks and a Spray & Go shop, 6 sequential story missions, side activities (taxi fares, rampage bonuses, 20 hidden packages, stunt jumps), mouse-aim combat with three purchasable weapons, shops and a car dealer, a full HUD with minimap, a day/night cycle with rain, procedural WebAudio sound and music, and a localStorage save. Every name, character, vehicle, story, and sound is original.

## Controls

WASD / Arrow keys — Move & drive · Shift — Sprint · E — Enter / exit car · Mouse — Aim, click — Shoot · 1–4 — Switch weapon · F — Interact (Spray & Go, repair, armor, save) — Pause · M — Mute

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — or open `game/index.html` directly in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests, everything plays offline from the static files under `game/`.
